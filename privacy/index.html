Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

The AWS Student Builder Group — AIUB is an independent student-led university community at the American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). We respect the privacy of all student members, attendees, and visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is safeguarded.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our chapter—such as registering for workshops, joining study jams, or connecting to our community channels—we may collect minimal necessary information, including:

  • Contact Information: Your full name, AIUB student email (e.g. @student.aiub.edu), and student ID for university event verification.
  • Academic Details: Department (e.g. CSE, EEE, CSSE) and academic semester to tailor relevant cloud learning tracks.
  • Event RSVP Data: Session interests, lab attendance records, and certification goal preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

All information collected is used strictly for non-commercial, educational community purposes:

  • Notifying you about upcoming AIUB campus workshops, hackathons, and guest lectures.
  • Issuing AWS Skill Builder lab credentials, sandbox access, or event attendance certificates.
  • Coordinating certification cohorts and exam voucher distribution.
  • We never sell, rent, or monetize student data under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

Our website and communication channels contain links to external platforms, such as the official AWS Builder Center, our Official Facebook Page, our Meetup group, and AIUB official portals. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain participant information only for as long as necessary to conduct the educational activities of the chapter. You may request deletion or correction of your registration details at any time by contacting the student executive committee.

6. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or chapter data practices, reach out via our official Facebook community page: facebook.com/AWSBuildersAIUB or connect with the chapter leads on the AIUB Permanent Campus in Kuratoli, Dhaka.
