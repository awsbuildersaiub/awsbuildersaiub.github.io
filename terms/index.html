Community Guidelines & Legal

Terms & Conditions

Effective date: October 2026

1. Chapter Status & Affiliation

The AWS Student Builder Group — AIUB is an independent, student-run technical community operated by students of American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). While our activities are supported by Amazon Web Services student community initiatives, we are an independent campus organization and not an official legal entity of Amazon.com, Inc. or AIUB administration.

2. Eligibility & Membership

Membership is free and open to all actively enrolled undergraduate and graduate students of AIUB across all departments (including CSE, CSSE, SE, EEE, CoE, BBA, and related disciplines). Members must maintain academic honesty and follow the university's student code of conduct.

3. Code of Conduct & Inclusive Environment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free environment for all student builders, regardless of experience level, gender, academic standing, or background. We expect all attendees and members to:

  • Be respectful, collaborative, and constructive during study jams and social discussions.
  • Encourage peer learning and assist fellow AIUBians who are new to cloud computing.
  • Report any disruptive or inappropriate behavior immediately to community leads.

4. Responsible Use of Cloud Sandboxes & Resources

Temporary AWS sandbox credentials, credits, or workshop accounts provided through the chapter must be used strictly for educational workshop objectives. Prohibited actions include:

  • Cryptocurrency mining, stress-testing, or launching unauthorized network traffic.
  • Storing private personal data or deploying production commercial systems on student sandbox accounts.
  • Sharing or transferring assigned credentials outside authorized event attendees.

5. Intellectual Property

Students retain full ownership of any code, capstone projects, or architectures they build during chapter hackathons and study jams. Open-source contributions are encouraged under standard permissive licenses (MIT / Apache 2.0).

6. Updates & Inquiries

These Terms & Conditions may be updated periodically to reflect chapter growth and event guidelines. Stay connected and message our executive leads through our official Facebook page: facebook.com/AWSBuildersAIUB.
